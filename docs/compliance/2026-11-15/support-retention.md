# Support correspondence retention

Adopted by the operator on November 15, 2026: retain Z8 support correspondence
while handling the inquiry, then delete it **90 days after resolution**. The
[English](../../privacy.html) and [Korean](../../privacy.ko.html) policies state
this rule. It applies to operator-held support mail, replies, attachments and
working copies, not the user's project files or external provider records.

## Procedure

1. Identify Z8 support correspondence separately from unrelated personal mail.
   Keep the resolution date and the deletion due date (resolution plus 90 days)
   in a private support record. Do not put messages or addresses in this repo.
2. Check due records daily. On the due date, permanently delete the relevant
   received and sent correspondence, attachments and downloaded working copies.
   Moving mail to Trash alone is insufficient. Delete only the relevant support
   messages; never empty an entire mixed personal mailbox as a shortcut.
3. Remove the case-linked retention record with the correspondence. Any retained
   operational totals should not identify the sender or preserve the message.
4. Handle access, correction and early deletion requests through the published
   privacy contact. Verify the requester using no more information than needed;
   ordinary support does not require a government-ID copy or CLI credentials.
5. If a specific legal preservation duty arises, record the actual legal basis,
   affected information and required duration, and update the applicable notice.
   Do not invent a standing legal-retention exception for ordinary support.

This is a manual operating procedure, not a running Gmail automation. No mailbox
was read, changed or purged during this review. The operator must carry out the
procedure. A later automation needs its own narrowly scoped implementation and
authorization; adopting the retention rule does not authorize bulk deletion of
unrelated mail.

The operator separately confirmed on November 15 that this is ordinary free
Gmail and that there is no automatic forwarding to another address. This does
not establish a Workspace processing contract or a restricted storage region.

Google describes separate deletion and backup processing that can outlast a
user's deletion action. The public policy therefore distinguishes deletion of
operator-controlled support records from provider-held backups and the sender's
copies. See [Google retention](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention?hl=en).

This procedure closes the previously unspecified operator retention period. It
does not establish the appropriate legal basis, country/recipient disclosures
or safeguards for every cross-border processing arrangement; see the
[privacy assessment](privacy-review.md).
